Reaction start: keep off the throttle while the five red lights come on, and hit it the moment they go out. Your reaction time is shown. Touch the throttle too early and it's a jump start: 5-second penalty and lap 1 deleted. Leaving the track with all four wheels deletes the lap (track limits). Sectors and mini-sectors light up purple for your fastest ever, green for your best this race and yellow when slower. Graphics are on Auto by default and only lower detail if your computer can't keep up. Pick High or Ultra on the Grand Prix screen to keep it sharp. In Time Trial you chase your personal best against your ghost. In Competitive you race 21 AI drivers. Pick Easy, Medium, Hard or Expert, choose your grid slot (P1 to P22, or Random) on the Grand Prix screen, then fight your way forward and score points: 25 for a win, down to 1 for 10th.
