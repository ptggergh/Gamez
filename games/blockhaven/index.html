How to play

W A S DWalk. Double-tap W or hold R to sprint. MouseLook around. Click the world to lock your mouse to the game. Left clickHold to mine a block. Click a mob to hit it. Right clickPlace a block, open tables, furnaces and chests. Hold to eat food. SpaceJump and swim up. In creative, double-tap to fly. ShiftSneak so you don't fall off edges. Fly down in creative. 1–9 / wheelPick a hotbar slot. EOpen your inventory and 2×2 crafting grid. QDrop the held item. Middle clickCopy the block you're looking at (creative). F3Show coordinates and frame rate. EscFree your mouse, pause and save.

First night: punch a tree for logs, turn them into planks, make a crafting table, then a wooden pickaxe. Coal plus a stick makes torches.
